The Problem & Solution

Why this B.Tech project matters

The Invisible Threat

Microplastics are plastic fragments less than 5mm in length. They are becoming a pervasive environmental hazard, entering our oceans, food chain, and even drinking water.


Our Approach

Manual counting of microplastics under a microscope is tedious and error-prone. Our project automates this using Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN). By uploading a microscopic image of a water sample, our system classifies and counts plastic particles in real-time.
